How to play

  • ✦ Place : click anywhere to scatter a glowing star.
  • ⟍ Connect : click two stars to draw a line between them, then name your shape.
  • ☄ Wish : send a shooting star streaking across your sky.
  • ✯ Auto Star : drop a ready made five point star.
  • ✦ Galaxy : spin up a swirling spiral of stars.
  • ☁︎ Sky : cycle the background through different cosmic moods.
  • ⟲ Clear : wipe the sky and begin again.
  • ⤓ Save : download your sky as an image.

Tip: your sky saves itself automatically, so close the tab and it will be waiting.
